Install Nodio
=============

1. Drag Nodio to the Applications folder.
2. Open Nodio from Applications.


If macOS says "Nodio is damaged" or "Nodio cannot be opened"
------------------------------------------------------------

Nodio is not from the App Store, so macOS can block it. Do one of these:

A. Double-click "Fix Nodio.terminal" in this window.

B. Open Terminal, paste this command, and press Return:

xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Nodio.app

Then open Nodio again.


First launch
------------

Nodio asks for Microphone and Accessibility access. Follow the setup guide.
The speech model downloads one time. Then hold Fn and speak.
