Features

Built for speed and privacy.

keyboard

Global hotkey

Press the Fn / Globe key on your Mac keyboard to start recording. Release to stop and transcribe. Works system-wide, no app switching.

mic

On-device recognition

Apple's SFSpeechRecognizer runs entirely on your Mac. Audio never leaves your device. Zero latency, zero cloud, zero privacy concerns.

content_paste

Smart text delivery

Nodio injects text directly into the focused input field using the Accessibility API. If no field is focused, it copies to your clipboard.

graphic_eq

Audio enhancement

A noise gate suppresses background sound and peak normalization boosts your voice before transcription, improving accuracy in noisy environments.

visibility

Floating HUD

A compact, always-on-top pill with live waveform bars shows you Nodio is listening. No windows, no distractions — just a visual pulse.

content_copy

Copy last message

Need to re-use a transcription? The menu bar keeps your last message one click away. Copy it instantly from the Nodio menu.

How it works

Three steps. That's it.

1

Hold Fn to record

Press and hold the Fn / Globe key on your Mac. The floating pill appears with a live waveform — Nodio is listening.

2

Release to transcribe

Let go of Fn. Nodio applies a noise gate and normalization, then runs on-device speech recognition on the enhanced audio.

3

Text lands where you are

The transcription is injected at your cursor in the focused text field. No field? It copies to your clipboard with a confirmation sound.

shield

Your voice stays on your Mac.

Nodio uses Apple's native on-device speech recognition. Audio is processed locally, enhanced locally, and discarded after transcription. No servers, no telemetry, no accounts.
