How it works.

  1. Hold Fn.

    A small pill appears at the top of the screen and shows your words as you speak.

  2. Speak.

    Whisper decodes your audio on the Mac every 1.2 seconds while you talk.

  3. Release.

    A last full pass runs, then commands, snippets, and the app style apply.

  4. Done.

    The text goes in at your cursor. If there is no text field, it goes on your clipboard.

Prefer another key? Use Right Option, Right Command, or Right Control. Key combos such as ⌘C never start a recording.

Set up in a few minutes.

  1. Download and drag to Applications.

    Get Nodio.dmg from GitHub, open it, and drag Nodio into Applications.

    download Download for Mac

  2. Open it the first time.

    Nodio is not from the App Store, so macOS asks before it opens. What you see depends on your macOS version.

    The dialog has an Open button

    Control-click Nodio in Applications, choose Open, then click Open again. You do this one time.

    The dialog only has Move to Trash

    Do not click it. Open Terminal and run this command, then open Nodio again:

    xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Nodio.app

  3. Follow the setup guide.

    On first launch, a window walks you through three steps. It shows a check mark for each one when it is done.

    • Microphone. So Nodio can hear you.
    • Accessibility. So Nodio can see the hotkey and type into apps.
    • Speech model. Downloads by itself and shows progress. You can close the window, and Nodio tells you when it is ready.

    While the model downloads, the menu bar icon shows a download arrow. Open the guide again at any time from the menu: Setup Guide.

  4. Hold Fn and talk.

    Click in any text field and try it. Open Settings from the menu bar to pick a model, a hotkey, or a language.

Questions.

Does Nodio need the internet?

Only to download the model one time and to check GitHub for updates. Dictation itself works offline.

Why only Indian English?

Nodio is tuned for one accent so it can do it well. The prompt, the default model, and Hinglish mode all target Indian English speakers. Other English accents still work, with less tuning.

Which Mac do I need?

macOS 13.3 or later. Any Apple silicon Mac runs Large v3 Turbo well. On Intel Macs, pick the Small model in Settings.

Where are my dictations stored?

The last 1000 are in ~/Library/Application Support/Nodio on your Mac. Delete one, or all of them, from the History tab.

Can I see the words before they go in?

Yes. The pill shows them while you speak, and the text goes in when you release. Turn on Live Typing if you want words typed as you talk.

Is it free?

Yes. Nodio is MIT licensed, and the source is on GitHub.
